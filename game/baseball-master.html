How to Play Arcade Baseball

1. Batter POV & 4 Strike Zones

Stand at home plate facing the pitcher. As the pitch travels toward you into 1 of 4 strike zones (Top, Left, Right, Bottom), swing matching the target zone!

2. Controls & Timing

  • W or Up Arrow: Top Zone
  • A or Left Arrow: Left Zone
  • D or Right Arrow: Right Zone
  • S or Down Arrow: Bottom Zone
  • Touch Screen / Mouse: Tap the corresponding directional button overlay.

3. Official Scoring & Rules

  • Base Hit (< Stadium HR Dist): Points = Distance ÷ 2. No swing lost! HR Streak resets.
  • Home Run (>= Stadium HR Dist): Points = Distance + previous HR streak points! Earn +1 Bonus Swing!
  • Miss / Strike: Incorrect zone or timing = Lose 1 Swing. HR Streak resets.
