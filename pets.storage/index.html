Privacy Policy for MarbleVet Journal

Effective Date: November 10, 2026  |  App: MarbleVet Journal

1. Introduction and Overview

Welcome to MarbleVet Journal. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we collect, store, manage, and protect your information when you use our web application. We believe in straightforward data handling, ensuring that you understand exactly what happens to your information when you upload pet records, participate in discussions, or manage your account.

2. Information We Collect and Purpose of Collection

  • Account Credentials (Username & Password): When you register, we collect and save your chosen username and account password. This data is strictly used to create your unique user account, authenticate your login sessions, assign ownership to your posts, and grant you secure access to the app.
  • Written Entry Text (Titles & Descriptions): We save all text entries you create, including post titles, pet health notes, symptom descriptions, and community questions. This text is stored so you can view your personal pet health logs and so other community members can read your public posts.
  • File Uploads (Photos, Videos, Audio, and Documents): When you attach files—such as photos, video clips, audio recordings, or PDF documents—we store these uploads in our cloud database (pet-attachments bucket). Saving these files allows the platform to display your attached media and enforce visibility controls depending on whether an entry is set to private or public.
  • Community Replies & Comments: When you post a response or advice on a public community thread, we collect and save the text of your reply. Storing replies enables crowdsourced pet care discussions and keeps the community advice hub visible to all platform users.

3. Data Visibility and Access Controls

  • Private Pet Health Logs: Entries marked as private are restricted strictly to your account view within the application interface. Other regular users cannot see or search your private medical notes or documents.
  • Public Community Hub Posts: Entries marked for community or public view, along with associated replies, can be read by all authenticated users signed into the platform.
  • Administrator & Infrastructure Access: Because user accounts, posts, comments, and media files are stored backend in Supabase, authorized platform administrators have direct back-end access to the database records and file storage buckets.

4. Data Retention, Deletion, and Limitations

  • User Content Removal: You retain control over your posts and comments and can delete your own content through the app interface.
  • Administrative Moderation: Platform administrators reserve the right to remove any post, reply, file attachment, or user account to enforce community guidelines or resolve technical issues.
  • Front-End vs. Back-End Removal: When content is deleted through the front-end user interface, it is immediately hidden and removed from the active view of all users. However, lingering records, cache copies, or database backups directly within Supabase backend storage may persist temporarily or permanently depending on database archive rules.

5. What We Never Collect

  • Location Data: We do not track, request, or store your GPS, IP-based geographic location, or physical address.
  • Real Names: We do not require you to provide your legal real name. You may use any unique username.
  • Unnecessary Personal Identifiers: We do not ask users to submit sensitive personal background information, phone numbers, or unrelated demographic data.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our app features or backend infrastructure. Continued use of MarbleVet Journal constitutes acceptance of the current policy.
